# Adrik Scout rulebook

Every lesson learned from a mosque website, written as a **general rule** that applies to every mosque — including mosques added later.
Generated from `supabase/functions/scout/rules.ts` (the single source of truth) and `scripts/browser-scout/answer-keys.json`; do not edit by hand.

**Adding a lesson.** When a site is read wrong: (1) write the general rule in `rules.ts` — never hand-enter that mosque's times; (2) add an answer key (or a screenshot fixture) that would have caught it and list the rule id in its `rules`; (3) push. Every push re-runs all answer keys and prints each rule as passing (✓), failing (✗) or untested (·).

**62 rules**: 27 reading a schedule · 7 checks in code · 8 finding the times (plain reader) · 10 reading in a real browser · 10 keeping the rules honest.

## Reading a schedule

Sent to Claude, word for word, as its reading instructions for every mosque.

| `iqama-not-adhan` | Iqama ≠ adhan. If a table has both ADHAN and IQAMA columns, extract IQAMA. Columns called "Begins", "Starts", "Start" or "Athan" are adhan; "Iqama", "Iqamah", "Jamaat", "Jama'ah", "Congregation" are iqama. | `arrow-change` | Time changes written as "X --> Y", "X → Y" or "X to Y" (e.g. at a DST change) mean the iqama moved from X to Y: Y is the current time. | `feed-placeholders` | Data feeds and widgets: "00:00", "--:--" or an empty value is a placeholder, never an iqama time. | `mawaqit-offset` | "N min after adhan" (Mawaqit "+N"): return a rule — offset_from_adhan with minutes=N; for Maghrib use sunset_offset with minutes=N. Fixed times in a per-day calendar go in months as rows. | Anatolia Islamic Centre (Mawaqit: Asr +5, Maghrib +3) | Anatolia Islamic Centre |
| `tomorrow-line` | A "Tomorrow" line under a prayer (or a "tomorrow's iqama change" note) gives that prayer's iqama for the NEXT date: today's value is the one in the prayer's own row; return the tomorrow value as a row for tomorrow's date. In flattened text like "MAGHRIB / TOMORROW 5:07 5:12 / 5:11", 5:07 is the start, 5:12 today's iqama and 5:11 tomorrow's. | Rowdah Centre (prayertimedisplay.com widget, red 'Tomorrow' line) | Rowdah Centre, Masjid Al-Salam |
| `shown-date` | A page or screenshot that displays a date: its times belong to that date, not to the day it was read or received (widgets follow the reader's clock, so a late-evening read can already show tomorrow). A screenshot from a community member shows one day. | Screenshot inbox (BARIC, ISNA); BARIC's widget showing the next day's date and times when read late at night | Brampton and Regional Islamic Centre (BARIC) (screenshot), ISNA Canada (Islamic Centre of Canada) (screenshot), Mississauga Muslim Community Center (MMCC) (screenshot), ICCO MAC Masjid (screenshot), Huttonville Jame Masjid (screenshot), Canadian Islamic Union (CIU) (screenshot) |
| `ignore-city-widgets` | Screenshots: when page screenshots or timetable images are attached, read them as part of the page and prefer them over garbled text. Ignore generic city prayer-time widgets (e.g. "Toronto prayer times") that are not this mosque's iqama. | Ar-Rashaad (timesprayer.com Toronto widget beside the real block), Masjid Mubarak (Vaughan city table) | Ar-Rashaad Centre, Masjid Mubarak (Ahmadiyya) |
| `pdf-year-columns` | An attached PDF is a timetable the mosque links to: read it as part of the page, check which year it is for, and whether its columns are iqama or prayer-start times. A perpetual or past-year calculated timetable is adhan times, not iqama. | Islamic Forum of Canada (2011 perpetual start-time PDF) | Islamic Forum of Canada |
| `dates-beat-titles` | When a timetable's heading contradicts its own rows, the rows win: a table headed "Ramadan" (or with an old year in its title or file name) whose rows are dated for the current or coming months is the current schedule, not a past one. A table with no year whose months are this month and the next ones applies to this season. Judge a timetable by its dates, not its label. | Huttonville Mosque (wphc.ca: the winter Nov–Feb iqama table sits under a 'Ramadan Prayer Timings' heading, and the menu links to the real Ramadan 2025 table; Wael spotted the typo) | Huttonville Jame Masjid, Huttonville Jame Masjid (screenshot) |
| `shared-site` | A website that serves several places (a centre that runs a mosque and a musalla): daily times apply only to the place the site says holds daily prayers. A place the site lists only with Jumu'ah times gets its Jumu'ah slots and no daily times. | wphc.ca (operates Huttonville Mosque 'for regular and Juma prayers'; lists Ha-Meem Musalla with Jumu'ah times only) | Huttonville Jame Masjid, Ha-Meem Musalla (Peace and Harmony Centre) |
| `location-picker` | Sites with several locations or a "Change location" picker: use the times for this mosque's own location. A mosque's own website often names its centre differently from our list (the organization's name vs. the centre's name, an acronym vs. the full name), so on the mosque's own site the location shown by default IS this mosque — unless the page clearly names a different city or address than the one given for this mosque. Only then return no times. | ISNA Canada (site shows its centre 'Islamic Centre of Canada (ICC)' with a location picker; a stricter first version of this rule wrongly returned nothing — caught by the screenshot fixture) | ISNA Canada (Islamic Centre of Canada) (screenshot) |
| `facilities-explicit` | Extract facilities only if explicitly stated. Otherwise null. | Scout design | — |
| `honest-confidence` | confidence is your honest 0-1 estimate that every returned time matches the page. | Scout design | — |

## Checks in code

Enforced by the scout edge function after Claude has read the page, whatever Claude concluded.

| Rule | What it says | Learned from | Tested by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `adhan-check`<br><sub>index.ts adhanVerdict()</sub> | Adhan vs iqama is decided by arithmetic, not by the page's label: compare Fajr/Dhuhr/Asr/Isha with the calculated adhan for the mosque's location and date. ≥60% within 3 min → adhan (held back); ≤25% → iqama; in between, Claude's reading decides. | Ar-Rashaad (wrongly held back because its block says 'Prayer Times'), Masjid Mubarak (correctly held back), Canadian Islamic Union (a yearly calendar labelled 'iqamah' that matches the calculated start times on 98% of days: held back) | Masjid-E-Aqsa, Ar-Rashaad Centre, Masjid Mubarak (Ahmadiyya), Islamic Forum of Canada, Nasfat Peel Region, Canadian Islamic Union (CIU), Canadian Islamic Union (CIU) (screenshot) |
| `iqama-never-before-adhan`<br><sub>index.ts adhanVerdict()</sub> | An iqama can never come before the adhan: any time more than 5 min before the earliest possible adhan (Shafi) marks the set as adhan/calculated times and holds it back. | Bab ul Ilm (Jafari Fajr before the ISNA adhan), other calculated-time pages | Bab ul Ilm - Bani Hashim Society |
| `maghrib-near-sunset`<br><sub>index.ts checkMaghrib()</sub> | A Maghrib jama'ah starts shortly after sunset. A fixed Maghrib time before sunset or more than 45 minutes after it is wrong for that date — typically a time the mosque did not update after a clock change — and is dropped instead of stored (sunset and sunset+N rules are unaffected). | Tawheed / Dar Al-Tawheed (themasjidapp widget still showing the summer Maghrib 6:15 pm after the November clock change; sunset 5:06) | — |
| `jumuah-shared-start`<br><sub>index.ts checkJumuah()</sub> | Two Jumu'ah slots cannot share one khutbah time: a khutbah time repeated across slots whose salah times differ is the day's prayer-start time, not a khutbah, so each slot keeps its salah time as its only time. | Malton Mosque (both slots stored with khutbah 12:02 — the Dhuhr start in the 'Begins' column — and salah 1:00 / 2:15) | Malton Mosque |
| `rule-replacement`<br><sub>index.ts store()</sub> | A new standing rule for a prayer ends the older open rule for that prayer the day before the new one starts — mosques change times, and the old rule must not linger. Likewise a re-read replaces the month's Jumu'ah slots: slots the page no longer lists are removed. | Al-Hikmah Centre (Asr moved 15:30 → 15:00 on 3 Nov) | — |
| `no-placeholder-rows`<br><sub>index.ts store()</sub> | "00:00" values are never stored as iqama rows. | Site data feeds with empty Maghrib | — |
| `review-low-confidence`<br><sub>index.ts store()</sub> | A read with confidence below 0.8, or held back as adhan, is flagged needs_review; held-back rows are quarantined and never shown in the app. | Scout design | — |

## Keeping the rules honest

How screenshots, answer keys and fixes work.

| Rule | What it says | Learned from | Tested by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `jamaah-only`<br><sub>app (guide) + index.ts quarantine</sub> | The app shows only jama'ah (iqama) times a mosque publishes. Prayer start times are never shown or counted as an iqama, even when a mosque publishes them as its "daily prayer times"; such a mosque is suggested only for the prayers it gives a jama'ah time for. | Islamic Forum of Canada (its Faisaliyyah Hanafi start-time PDF vs. its published Dhuhr iqama 1:30) — Wael's decision, 4 Nov 2026 | Nasfat Peel Region, Canadian Islamic Union (CIU) |
| `website-check`<br><sub>missing list (mosques never read, or whose site does not load)</sub> | A mosque that has never been read, or whose website does not load or is only an organization's national homepage, gets its website re-checked by name: websites on file can be wrong, outdated, or point at the parent organization. The corrected site then goes through every rule as usual. | Nasfat Peel Region (nasfatpeelregion.com on file does not exist; the real site is .ca), MAC Speakman (MAC's national homepage on file) | — |
| `jumuah-only-places`<br><sub>mosques.jumuah_only</sub> | A place that holds only Jumu'ah — its own site says so, its listing's name says so ("Jumuah only"), or a person who knows it confirms — is marked Jumu'ah-only: it is never suggested for the daily prayers and is not counted as missing them. | `duplicate-listing`<br><sub>mosques.active</sub> | Map listings can carry the same mosque twice (an old address, another name or organization). | `closed-listing`<br><sub>mosques.active</sub> | A map listing that a person who knows the community says holds no daily prayers (closed, moved, or not a prayer place) is turned off (active = false, reversible): it is not shown, not read, and not counted as missing. | MAC Mississauga (Speakman) — 2380 Speakman Dr; MAC lists no centre there, its Mississauga masjid is ICCO (Wael's decision, 4 Nov 2026) | — |
